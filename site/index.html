Healthcare Price Transparency

Public hospital rate lookup · healthcare.traqqit.com

Problem
Hospital negotiated-rate files (machine-readable MRF data) are huge and unusable for a patient or a small practice trying to compare prices.
Built
A DuckDB and Parquet pipeline that ingests and normalizes negotiated rates, with a FastAPI backend and a web front end for looking up rates by procedure and payer (initial focus: Anthem PPO).
Outcome
A working public lookup tool, and the groundwork for an orthopedics-focused variant.
  • Python
  • DuckDB
  • Parquet
  • FastAPI
  • Hostinger

Medical bill review automation

Data Hound Technologies · intake pipeline

Problem
Reviewing and negotiating medical bills is manual: read the bill, pull the codes, check them against expected rates, draft the dispute.
Built
n8n workflows with OCR that extract line items from uploaded bills and stage them for review and negotiation.

About

Before that I worked on ACO performance and population health at OnBelay Health Solutions, Equality Health, and ACO Partner, mostly in SQL, Python, and Power BI, mostly on claims.

On the side I run Data Hound Technologies, teach graduate health informatics at Grand Canyon University, and build small tools that live at traqqit.com. I like problems where the data is messy, the money is real, and someone has to explain the answer to a non-technical room.
