Healthcare Price Transparency

Public hospital rate lookup · healthcare.traqqit.com

Problem
Hospital negotiated-rate files (machine-readable MRF data) are huge and unusable for a patient or a small practice trying to compare prices.
Built
A DuckDB and Parquet pipeline that ingests and normalizes negotiated rates, with a FastAPI backend and a web front end for looking up rates by procedure and payer (initial focus: Anthem PPO).
Outcome
A working public lookup tool, and the groundwork for an orthopedics-focused variant.
  • Python
  • DuckDB
  • Parquet
  • FastAPI
  • Hostinger

Medical bill review automation
